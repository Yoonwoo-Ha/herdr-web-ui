# Terminal screen: visual differences from herdr

This file lists where Ovis's terminal screen still draws something other than herdr 0.9.5's own client for the same scene, with herdr's rule behind it, where the screen draws it, and why it differs. Everything else in the compared scenes matches herdr's client cell for cell (see "How it is checked"). The ids (S4, R7, C2, ...) are the ones the code and the tests cite. herdr's rules were read from its source (github.com/herdrdev/herdr, tag v0.9.5) and are written here in our own words; no herdr code is copied or translated.

herdr's colours below are its default theme's: accent `#89b4fa`, text `#cdd6f4`, subtext `#a6adc8`, overlay0 `#6c7086`, overlay1 `#7f849c`, panel background `#181825`, active row `#1e1e2e`, selection and surface0 `#313244`, red `#f38ba8`, yellow `#f9e2af`, teal `#94e2d5`, green `#a6e3a1`, mauve `#cba6f7`. "Default" means the host's own colour.

## How it is checked

herdr 0.9.5 ran with its own server in an isolated session (its defaults, except onboarding, update checks and sound off, and in-app toasts on for the agent scenes), on a pseudo-terminal behind an emulated host terminal that answers what a real one answers (device attributes, synchronized output, the default colours, the 256 palette entries, the colour scheme), once with a dark host (background `#1e1e1e`, text `#cccccc`) and once with a light one (background `#ffffff`, text `#333333`). `tui/testing/herdr-capture.ts` reads what herdr painted back as a cell grid (each cell's character, its foreground and background as written, and its attributes) and stores it under `tui/testing/herdr-captures/`. The scenes are those of `tui/testing/parity-scenes.ts`: the main view, the modes, the overlays, the narrow window, notices, an ended pane, an empty core, agents in every state, two viewers of one terminal, a git checkout (`git`), the right-click world (`rc`: a split tab and a second tab, an agent, a git checkout and a linked worktree) and a program that asks for the mouse (`mouse`), at 120x40, 80x24, 64x20 and 45x30 (the right-click worlds at the two wide sizes).

`tui/view/parity.test.ts` draws each scene through the screen's real loop on the fake core, reads the frames back through the same emulator, and compares every cell with herdr's capture: its text, its attributes, its background, its foreground where a glyph shows it, and the host cursor. herdr's picture is first carried through the lines below that stay different, each by its id (`decisionsFor` in `tui/testing/parity.ts`):

- C1: outside the sidebar herdr's palette is mapped onto the screen's theme roles (accent to accent, panel to panel, its greys of text to text and dim text, red to blocked, and so on), and where herdr writes no background the theme's own background counts as the same. Inside the sidebar and its rail every colour is compared as a value, herdr's no background as none; an overlay lying over the sidebar keeps the screen's look and is compared by role there. herdr's help scrollbar thumb and track, two greys, both map to the dim text.
- C2: where herdr writes one of its words the screen's own word is put in its place, in herdr's look for that word (the switcher's `Agents`, `Keyboard Shortcuts`, the help's `help`, `No agents`, `Rename tab`, `New tab name`, `Copied`; on the mode bar `navigate` and `help`). Those cells are compared on place, shape and colour role. The copy toast, narrower with `Copied`, is checked on its own. Where the screen's words for a state are longer than herdr's (the picker's state column, the narrow header's agent summary), those cells are left out.
- S8: outside the sidebar a state's glyph and its colour are left out of its cell; S7: the agent rows in the switcher. The sidebar has no hole.
- O7: the picker's foot shows the chosen terminal's path and folder, which name herdr's pane ids and its scratch folder: left out, with the end of its keys row, where herdr names its state filters.
- N2: the agent scenes were captured with herdr's toasts switched on; its card is left out, every other cell compared.
- In the agents world's switcher, the second row of a workspace: herdr names the program in its shown pane (a shell for the stand-in agents), the screen the agent it reports.
- R7: the rows of herdr's `New worktree` and `Delete worktree checkout...` are left out of the menus that hold them.
- A scene whose every cell another case compares already has no case: `settings` (O10), `viewers` (P6, no second viewer is drawn) and, at 64 columns or fewer, the collapsed scenes (no sidebar on either side). `LEFT_OUT` in `tui/testing/parity.ts` lists them with their reasons, and every case compares at least one cell.

herdr's dialogs keep the bold of the cell they are drawn over, and a dialog cell where only the character was written keeps that cell's whole look (how its toolkit paints, not a look of its own): that bold does not count in a dialog where the picture under it was bold, and such a cell is left out.

One viewer, or viewers of one size, hover as herdr's.

## Right-click menus

herdr 0.9.5's right-click menus were measured with the `rc` and `mouse` worlds at 120x40 and 80x24. The screen opens the same menus, at the pressed cell, with herdr's width, colours, keys and pointer, and each entry's action; the pane's `Send right-clicks to pane` is kept in Ovis's layout document beside the tab's zoom (parity.md). Two things differ: R7, and a right press read while a layout write is under way, which opens no menu and shows a `layout_busy` notice instead (parity.md).

| # | herdr | The screen | herdr's rule | In the screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A right press opens a menu on three targets only: a workspace row of the sidebar (its branch row too), a tab, and any cell of a pane's box (its text, its title and every border cell). Everywhere else nothing happens: the sidebar's header, blank rows, ` new`, `menu`, the rule, the agents' header and an agent's rows, `«`, the separator, the tab bar's `+` and empty end, and the rail of a collapsed sidebar. In prefix mode the bar drawn over a pane's bottom border counts as that pane; the menu opens and the prefix mode is still there once it closes. Over the help and the global menu a right press does nothing. Opening a menu changes no focus: a right-clicked pane, tab or workspace stays where it was. | The same. | The right press is matched against the sidebar's workspace rows (only when the sidebar is expanded), then the tabs, then the pane boxes (`src/client/shell/mouse.rs`); an open overlay takes the mouse first. | `mouse.ts` (`rightSite`), `tui/model/overlays.ts` |
| R2 | The menu is a box whose top left corner is the pressed cell, moved left and up just enough to fit the screen (at the bottom border it opens upward over the border; at 80 columns a pane menu near the right edge is shifted left over the neighbour). A right press on a pane's title does not copy the terminal's id in herdr: it opens the pane's menu (R1), nothing is written to the clipboard, and a left press on the title only focuses the pane.

## Already the same

Beyond the lines above, these match herdr and the parity cases keep them so: the tab bar on the first row right of the sidebar; side-by-side panes as separate boxes with touching corners; the accent on the focused pane's border; the ` Z` mark on a zoomed tab; the mode names PREFIX, NAVIGATE, RESIZE and COPY; the order in which a workspace's agents roll up into its state (blocked, done, working, idle); the cells inside a pane, which both leave exactly as the program wrote them outside a mouse selection (P8) and copy mode; and the host cursor at the program's own cursor in terminal mode.
